The Man Behind the Brand

From the cricket field to building Gwalior's premier sports destination

Yogendra Bhadoriya

🏏 Indian Cricketer & Founder

Born and raised in the heart of Gwalior, Yogendra Bhadoriya's journey from local cricket grounds to representing India is nothing short of inspirational. With years of experience at the highest level of competitive cricket, Yogendra understands what athletes truly need — quality, reliability, and the right gear to perform at their best.

Yogi Sports is his vision to bring world-class sports equipment to the athletes of Gwalior and beyond. Every product in the store is hand-picked by Yogendra himself, ensuring only the best reaches your hands.
